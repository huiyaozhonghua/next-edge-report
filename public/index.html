Hard gates and red lines
  • Developers must complete Pi KYC; every team member must register individually (or they are not eligible for prizes), and each person may use only one Pi account.
  • Registering means accepting the 22 rules of the Pi Next Hackathon 2026 Rules; you must be 18 or older; officials of the organiser and its parent or subsidiary companies — executives, employees, advisors, interns, contractors, judges and promotion agencies — and their immediate family are not eligible to participate.
  • Each team may submit up to 3 apps, and each must be a Mainnet Pi app or a SoloHost app; only one teammate submits each app.
  • Pi sign-in only, Pi payments only; no external redirects, no fiat or other tokens; the app must not become a funnnel to an outside platform.
  • Must have an English name and description, a compliant logo (must not start with "Pi" or use Pi's official logo and colours), a privacy policy, PiNet integration and preview images.
  • The demo video must be 3 minutes or less and publicly available (YouTube or similar), covering five things: what the app is, its features and interface, who it is for, the problem it solves, and how Pi is involved (Pi sign-in or Pi payments).
  • Before submitting you can enter the Midpoint submission for early feedback and exposure; the final deadline is 2027-01-05 23:59 PST = January 6, 15:59 Beijing time.
  • Never enter a recovery phrase or private key; the API Key lives only on the server, never in a conversation.
