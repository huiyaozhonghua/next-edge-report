Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

This app ("Next Edge Report") is a planning tool for Pi Next hackathon participants. It is built around Pi Network's identity infrastructure and is fully browsable without signing in. This policy explains exactly what data we handle.

Data we collect

  • Pi account identity: If you choose to sign in, we receive your Pi UID and username from Pi Authentication. We verify your access token against Pi Platform API on our server and use the server-verified UID as the key for your saved progress.
  • Access token: Used only for server-side verification against Pi's /me endpoint. It is not stored persistently after verification.
  • Your progress: The checkboxes you tick, the answers you give the topic matcher, and your check-in streak. This is stored in your browser and, when signed in, synced to your Pi account so it follows you across devices.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or browsing history outside this app. We do not require you to sign in to use any part of the app — the matcher, the 46-day plan, the research library and the check-in streak all work without a Pi account (PiNet-compatible).

How data is used

Identity data is used solely to sign you in and to associate your saved progress with your Pi account. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising.

Payments

This app does not accept payments and does not charge for anything. It contains no payment functionality of any kind.

Retention and security

Access tokens are used transiently for verification and are not retained. Saved progress is stored in our serverless backend (Cloudflare) under a key derived from your Pi UID, so it can be retrieved when you sign in again. Server-side credentials live only in the deployment environment's secret store and are never exposed to the client, written to logs, or committed to source control.

Your choices

You may browse and use the app without signing in. You may sign out at any time using the "Sign out" button. To request deletion of any associated data, contact the developer through the Pi ecosystem app directory.

Third-party services

This app relies on Pi Network's SDK and Platform API for authentication. Those services are governed by Pi Network's own terms and privacy practices. The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; Cloudflare processes the technical request data needed to serve the page.

Children

This app is intended for adults. Pi Network requires verified adult accounts, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version is always available at this URL.
