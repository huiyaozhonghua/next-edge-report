Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

This app ("Pi Next Report") presents a research report about the Pi Next hackathon. It is designed around Pi Network's identity and payment infrastructure. This policy explains what data we handle.

Data we collect

  • Pi account identity: When you choose to sign in, we receive your Pi UID and username from Pi Authentication. We verify your access token against Pi Platform API on our server and use the verified UID to maintain your session.
  • Access token: Used only for server-side verification against Pi's /me endpoint. It is not stored persistently after verification.
  • Payment information: If you make a support payment, we receive the payment identifier and amount from Pi Payments to confirm the transaction. We do not collect card, bank, or other financial details.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or browsing history outside this app. We do not require you to sign in to read the report — the report is fully browsable without a Pi account (PiNet-compatible).

How data is used

Identity data is used solely to sign you in and, if you choose, to confirm a support payment. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Retention and security

Access tokens are used transiently for verification and are not retained. Server-side secrets (PI_NETWORK_API_KEY) are kept only in server environment variables and are never exposed to the client or stored in logs.

Your choices

You may browse the app without signing in. You may sign out at any time using the "退出" button. To request deletion of any associated data, contact the developer through the Pi ecosystem app directory.

Third-party services

This app relies on Pi Network's SDK and Platform API for authentication and payments. Those services are governed by Pi Network's own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version is always available at this URL.
