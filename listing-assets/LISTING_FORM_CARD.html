Next Edge is a war room for Pi Next hackathon participants — not just another report. Answer 5 questions and the built-in matcher scores the best app ideas for YOUR skills, time, and assets. Then track a 46-day phased checklist and build a daily check-in streak. Progress saves automatically and syncs to your Pi account. Why it exists: 75% of the $200,000 USDT prize pool rewards real user engagement and retention. So we built a tool people come back to — not a one-time read. Features: - Topic matcher with a transparent weighted scoring engine (computed locally, nothing uploaded) - 46-day plan from eligibility to submission, with auto-saved progress - Daily check-in streak to keep you moving - Cross-verified research library: prizes, judging criteria, past winners, expected value, red lines - Original deep-dive: prize structure breakdown, hard entry thresholds, risk grading, and three low-cost topic directions Built for the Pi ecosystem: Pi sign-in, PiNet compatible.   5. 填 Demo video = https://youtu.be/VWwMu_VHQes
  6. 填 Team members（单人参赛填你自己）
  7. 提交上架审核 —— 官方明确不必等审核通过即可参赛
  8. 提交 Pi Next 参赛登记：在同一页面把本 App 登记进 "Your Pi Next apps"
    （官方：*"Registering alone doesn't enter your app."* 报名 ≠ 参赛）
